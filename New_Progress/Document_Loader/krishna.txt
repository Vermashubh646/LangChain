In the deepest hour of the darkest night
A child was born to bring the light
He wears the color of the monsoon sky
With a twinkle of mischief in his lotus eye
A peacock feather rests upon his hair
A beauty beyond what the earth can bear
Carried across the river in the storm
To take a humble cowherd's form
In Gokul’s dust he starts to crawl
The Lord of the universe, small and tall
He reaches for butter with sticky hands
The ruler of galaxies and distant lands
He breaks the pots and steals the cream
Like a waking soul stealing a dream
His anklets jingle as he runs away
Turning life into a divine play
Mother Yashoda chases with a stick
But he is too charming and he is too quick
She opens his mouth to see what he ate
And sees the stars and the wheel of fate
She sees the mountains and the oceans deep
And rocks the Universe into sleep
He grows to a boy with a bamboo flute
And when he plays, the world goes mute
The river Yamuna stops its flow
Just to hear the melody grow
The cows stand still with listening ears
The music washes away all fears
In the moonlit forest, the Gopis dance
Caught in the web of a divine romance
With Radha standing by his side
Where love and devotion have nowhere to hide
He lifts the mountain on a single finger
Protecting the souls who choose to linger
He dances on the serpent’s heavy head
Turning the poison into grace instead
But the playful days must fade away
For the Prince of Mathura has a part to play
He leaves the village and the simple life
To enter the world of politics and strife
He kills the tyrant to set them free
Fulfilling the ancient prophecy
He builds a city in the western sea
A fortress of gold and majesty
Yet he wears no crown upon his brow
He is the silence behind the vow
The years roll on to the field of war
Where brothers fight like never before
The armies gather on the dusty plain
Preparing for glory and for pain
He holds the reins of the white horses tight
Driving the chariot into the light
He sits with Arjuna in the grief and dread
While the vultures circle overhead
He stops the time to speak the truth
To the trembling heart of the warrior youth
He sings the song of the Gita loud
Piercing the veil of every cloud
You have a right to your duty alone
But the fruits of action are not your own
He shows the form of a billion suns
Where the river of time forever runs
The Creator, Destroyer, the Beginning and End
The Cosmic Father and the dearest Friend
The war is fought and the war is won
But his work on earth is never done
For wherever the heart is pure and true
He appears again in his shade of blue
He is the laughter and he is the tear
He is the conqueror of every fear
The stealer of butter, the driver of kings
The song that the very spirit sings
Krishna the jagged, Krishna the whole
The eternal anchor of the human soul
